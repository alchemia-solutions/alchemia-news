### Unveiling Phase Transformation Mechanism in BN from Large-Scale Machine Learning Molecular Dynamics
**Fonte:** ChemRxiv · **Termo:** molecular dynamics · [Link](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv.15008201/v1)
